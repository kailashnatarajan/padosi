Padosi · your neighbours are the network

Interactive prototype of the core loop: speak → triage → relay → respond. Runs entirely in your browser, no server.

📱 Resident phone

Airplane mode · No signal. Bluetooth + Wi-Fi Direct only

💻 Responder dashboard (laptop via Office Kit)

Alerts arrive sorted by urgency. A responder taps “On my way” and the resident’s phone is updated over the same relay.

Prototype note: triage here uses a lightweight on-page keyword model to demonstrate the flow. The planned build swaps this for on-device Whisper + a small quantised Gemma/Llama model, and relays over Android Nearby Connections. Location shown is a fixed demo point.
